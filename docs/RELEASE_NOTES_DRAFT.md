## Suggested title

Blood & Glory Revival V5 — Android 15 Compatibility (Experimental)

Independent, unofficial compatibility project for BLOOD & GLORY 1.1.6.

### Confirmed

- V5 LIGHT / WALLET_COMPAT launches on an Android 15 Redmi (tanzanite).
- Main menu, battles and normal in-game shop have been reported working.
- A copy of the currently installed APK and OBB was combined into XAPK and verified by SHA-256 using the export tool.

### Still experimental

- Clean XAPK installation has not been tested.
- Glu Points awards and persistence have not been confirmed.
- Full offline airplane-mode startup remains unconfirmed.
- Original Glu online offers are not restored.
- Android 15 may require ADB installation exception for old target SDK.
